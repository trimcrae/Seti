                                                   Abstract. We prove some conjectures of K. C. Au concerning the descents of cyclotomic levels in
                                                   certain sums over multiple polylogarithms. Via answers to a question of Au in some particular cases,
                                                   we also confirm Broadhurst’s conjecture on honorary multiple zeta values at even weights.

                                   THE MAGNETIC FIELD OF HD 101065

                                           SIDNEY C. WOLFF AND WENDY HÄGEN
                                        Institute for Astronomy, University of Hawaii
                                                    Received 1975 December 19

               Zeeman spectroscopic observations show that HD 101065 (Przybylski's star) has a magnetic field
           strength of about —2200 gauss. The limited observations available show no evidence of magnetic or
           spectrum variability. Observations of the Am star 8 Nor were made in order to determine whether sys-
           tematic errors were present in the determination of He, and these data yield an upper limit of 150 gauss for
           the field of 8 Nor.
           Key words: peculiar A star — magnetic fields — rare-earth elements

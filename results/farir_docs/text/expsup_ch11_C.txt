 The observations described in 
 Section IV.A.4 established
that flux densities greater than 10 Jy at 60 and 100 um are in
error of at least 30% at 60 um and up to 70% at 100 um because
the responsivity of the detectors varied with the total flux on
the detectors. This effect depends on the background as well as
on the brightness of the source itself. The photometry of any
source in a background greater than 10 MJy sr -1 at 60 and 100 
um is therefore suspect.

 Introduction 

 This chapter describes the transformation of the raw data
into its final forms. Section V.A provides a general 
overview
of the data reduction, while subsequent sections provide a more
detailed description. The relative and absolute calibration of
the data is discussed separately in Chapter VI .
The results of the analysis of the data processing are given in 
 Chapter VII .
An overall description of the survey, including sky coverage,
completeness and reliability, is given in
 Chapter VIII .

                                                                  ABSTRACT

      Radiative data for transitions of astrophysical interest in radioactive elements Ra I, Ra II, Ac I and Ac II have been computed using
      a semi-empirical relativistic Hartree-Fock approach including core polarization eﬀects. Most of the oscillator strengths reported in
      the present paper are new. A possible detection of radium (Ra II) and actinium (Ac II) in the spectrum of the stars HD 101065 and
      HR 465 is examined and discussed.
      Key words. atomic data – stars: chemically peculiar

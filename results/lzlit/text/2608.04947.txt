                                            1. Introduction
   Continuity bounds quantify the stability of information measures under perturbations of the underlying
state. The study began with Fannes’ inequality and the sharp entropy bound of Audenaert [Fan73, Aud07].
For quantum conditional entropy, Alicki and Fannes obtained the first dimensionally useful estimate,
which was later substantially strengthened by Winter [AF04, Win16]. Sharp bounds were subsequently
obtained in the classical setting and when the conditioning system is classical [AS20, Wil20]. For fully
quantum states, the sharp continuity bound was first established under the fixed-marginal assumption
ρB = σB in the independent works [BLT25, ABD+ 25]; the unrestricted case, together with an effective-
dimension refinement, was resolved recently in [BCRK+ 26].
   Conditional Rényi entropies are parametric generalizations of the conditional quantum entropy. They
are operationally relevant because they characterize error and strong-converse exponents in several
quantum-information tasks. Petz–Rényi conditional entropies of orders α ∈ [ 12 , 1) appear in direct error-
exponent analyses for classical data compression with quantum side information [CHDH21, Ren25, CL25]
and, more recently, for quantum state merging, entanglement distillation, and quantum communication
[BCY26]. Sandwiched Rényi quantities of orders α ∈ [ 12 , 1) characterize reliability and strong-converse
exponents in quantum decoupling, privacy amplification, and related information-processing tasks
[LY24b, LY24a, BCY26, CDG24, RT26]. Sharp continuity bounds thus provide robustness estimates for
quantities that directly govern the exponential behavior of these protocols.
   For Rényi conditional entropy, Jabbour and Datta proved the sharp continuity bound for classical
distributions and for a classical conditioning system, throughout 0 ≤ α < 1 [JD22]. For fully quantum
states, continuity bounds for the sandwiched Rényi conditional entropy for α ≥ 1/2 and related quantities
were studied in [MD22, BG23, BCGM26]. In this paper, we determine the sharp fully quantum continuity
bounds for both the optimized Petz and sandwiched Rényi conditional entropies for every α ∈ [ 12 , 1).

7.3. Orders below one half. The fully quantum proof relies on the facts that x1/α is operator convex
and that x(1−α)/α is both operator monotone and operator concave. These properties hold in the required
ranges precisely when α ∈ [1/2, 1), and are used in Lemma A.1 and Theorem A.2. For α < 1/2, the
corresponding powers leave these operator ranges, so the present proof does not extend. Whether the
same sharp continuity modulus remains valid for 0 < α < 1/2 is left open.

7.5. Other conditional Rényi entropies. Sharp unrestricted continuity bounds remain open for the

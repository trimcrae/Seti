                                                           We report the first dark matter search results using the commissioning data from PandaX-
                                                         4T. Using a time projection chamber with 3.7-tonne of liquid xenon target and an exposure of
                                                         0.63 tonne·year, 1058 candidate events are identified within an approximate nuclear recoil energy
                                                         window between 5 and 100 keV. No significant excess over background is observed. Our data set
                                                         a stringent limit to the dark matter-nucleon spin-independent interactions, with a lowest excluded
                                                         cross section (90% C.L.) of 3.8 × 10−47 cm2 at a dark matter mass of 40 GeV/c2 .

                                         A PQ-Symmetric High-Scale SUSY Interpretation of the LZ High-Energy Recoil
                                                                                                    Wen Yin1
                                                                       1
                                                                           Department of Physics, Tokyo Metropolitan University,
                                                                           Minami-Osawa, Hachioji-shi, Tokyo 192-0397, Japan
                                                      The 248-keV nuclear-recoil-like event reported by LZ can be interpreted as near-threshold up-
                                                   scattering of 1.08-TeV thermal Higgsino dark matter. A neutral-Higgsino splitting of order 0.3–0.35
                                                   MeV places the fixed weak-interaction cross section close to the observed LZ interval and implies
                                                   electroweak gauginos at O(107 ) GeV. I point out that this spectrum is compatible with gravity-
                                                   mediated high-scale supersymmetry and a Kim–Nilles Peccei–Quinn (PQ) sector. Although the
                                                   GUT scale is predicted to be lower than the conventional one, proton decay is suppressed due to
                                                   the high SUSY scale which also solves the conventional CP, flavor, gravitino and moduli problems.
                                                   Indeed, proton decay may be probed in the future. The PQ symmetry lowers the Higgsino mass
                                                   below the SUSY scale and also yields a QCD axion that solves the strong-CP problem but with a
                                                   cancelled photon coupling due to the Higgsino contribution. If the axion and Higgsino constitute
                                                   mixed dark matter, the halo-profile-dependent H.E.S.S. limit can be relaxed.

                                                                                       A Zoology of Quantum Turing Patterns

                                                                                                        Kazuki Ikeda       1, 2, ∗
                                                                    1 Department of Physics, University of Massachusetts Boston, Boston, MA 02125, USA
                                                                              2 Center for Nuclear Theory, Department of Physics and Astronomy,

                                                             We explore quantum Turing pattern zoology, where the same Lindblad equation supports a morphology atlas
                                                          of stripes, spots, holes, labyrinths, and defects. The stable stripe species provides a quantitatively controlled case
                                                          in which morphology and Gaussian witness loss separate parametrically. In particular, visible Turing stripes can
                                                          remain after two Gaussian witness margins associated with the same 𝑘 ∗ mode cross zero in a completely positive
                                                          Lindblad lattice. The witness thresholds on the exact shell fall as N −1 . The stripe nematic threshold tends to a
                                                          nonzero value at fixed lattice size, time window, and morphology criterion. The ratio of the morphology threshold
                                                          to either witness threshold therefore grows with N . Imaging and momentum-resolved covariance measurements
                                                          probe these sectors separately.

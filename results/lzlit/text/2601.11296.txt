                 We report on a blinded search for dark matter (DM) using ionization-only (S2-only) signals in
               XENONnT with a total exposure of 7.83 tonne × year over 579 days in three science runs. Dedicated
               background suppression techniques and the first complete S2-only background model in XENONnT
               provide sensitivity to nuclear recoils of [0.5, 5.0] keVnr and electronic recoils of [0.04, 0.7] keVee .
               No significant excess over the expected background is observed, and we set 90% confidence level
               upper limits on spin-independent DM–nucleon and spin-dependent DM–neutron scattering for DM
               masses between 3 and 8 GeV/c2 , as well as on DM–electron scattering, axion-like particles, and
               dark photons, improving on previous constraints. For spin-independent DM–nucleon scattering, we
               exclude cross sections above 6.0 × 10−45 cm2 at a DM mass of 5 GeV/c2 , pushing the XENONnT
               sensitivity closer to the region where coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering (CEνNS) becomes
               an irreducible background.

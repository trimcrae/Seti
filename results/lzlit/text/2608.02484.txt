                                                             and anti-dipole hemispheres (+1.55), directly contradicting the SRS26 anisotropy argument”. Here
                                                             SRS26 refers to our analysis [2] which showed that “locally q0 has a strong dipole anisotropy aligned
                                                             approximately with the bulk flow, and only a small monopole component remains at distances
                                                             exceeding a few hundred Mpc”. We find that these authors confused Equatorial with Galactic
                                                             coordinates, thereby getting the CMB dipole direction wrong. Hence their conclusion is baseless.

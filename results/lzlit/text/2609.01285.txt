                                         A BSTRACT: We update our analyses concerning the non-resonant (Drell-Söding) contri-
                                         bution to the reaction γ(∗) p → π + π − p, now including general vertex functions for the
                                         coupling of pomeron, f 2R , and ρR , to pions. This takes into account the off-shellness of
                                         the pions. We present a comparison of our tensor-pomeron model, including our new
                                         Drell-Söding and ρ, ω vector-meson contributions, with data for the γp → π + π − p reac-
                                         tion measured by the ZEUS and H1 Collaborations. We find very good agreement of our
                                         theory with the experimental data for the π + π − invariant mass (Mππ ) and for angular
                                         distributions. Here we restrict our analysis to the region Mππ ≲ 1.2 GeV. For higher val-
                                         ues of Mππ we will have to consider production of higher-mass mesons. The purpose of
                                         the present article is to show that our tensor-pomeron model gives a very good fit to the
                                         so-called skewing of the ρ-resonance contribution in photoproduction. In fact, it is an old
                                         problem/puzzle to understood the difference of the shapes of the Mππ distributions in the
                                         region of the ρ(770) resonance in e+ e− annihilation and photoproduction. We think that
                                         with our results we have obtained for this problem a solution which is very satisfactory
                                         from the Quantum-Field-Theory point of view. Our results are also relevant for central-
                                         exclusive π + π − production in ultraperipheral pp, Ap, and AA collisions at the LHC and
                                         for photo- and electroproduction of π + π − at future electron-proton/ion colliders, EIC
                                         and LeHC.

1   Introduction

In this note we continue our investigation of the production of π + π − pairs in diffractive
photon-proton collisions. In [1] we have given a detailed discussion of this reaction for
real and slightly virtual photons at high energies, using and extending the results of [2]. In
particular, we have presented in [1] a Quantum-Field-Theory (QFT) treatment of the Drell-
Söding (DS) term [3–5], the non-resonant π + π − production. Here we consider again the
following reaction for real and slightly virtual photons

                     γ(∗) (q, µ) + p( p, s) → π + (k1 ) + π − (k2 ) + p( p′ , s′ ) ,
                     k = k1 + k2 .                                                        (1.1)

Here q, p, p′ , k1 , k2 are the momenta of the particles, k is the four-momentum of the π + π −
system, s, s′ are the spin indices of the protons, and µ is the vector index of the photon;
see (2.1) of [1]. We require 0 ⩽ −q2 ≲ 0.5 GeV2 . The diagrams for the DS term are shown
in figure 1 of [1]. To make the present article self contained we reproduce this figure here
(see figure 1).
     Note that in the diagrams (a) and (b) of figure 1 we have the full γππ vertex func-
tions and the full pion propagators. Using the generalized Ward identity for the pions we
showed in [1] that for real photons the product of these two factors in the diagrams of
figures 1(a) and 1(b) is fixed up to an irrelevant gauge term; see (A.27) and (A.28) of [1].
For small virtuality of the photons we made the reasonable ansatz for this product, (A.32)
in [1]. Let us now consider the pomeron couplings in figures 1(a) and 1(b). We use the
framework of the tensor-pomeron model [6]. The Ppp coupling is here for on-shell pro-
tons and is well known, as is the effective pomeron propagator; see (3.10), (3.11), (3.43),
and (3.44) of [6]. Also the Pππ vertex function is well known for on-shell pions; see (3.45)
and (3.46) of [6]. But in figures 1(a) and 1(b) we have the Pππ vertices with one pion being
off shell. In [1] we took also for this case the same Pππ vertex as for both pions on shell.

In the present paper we generalize our model by allowing for off-shell effects in the Pππ,
f 2R ππ, and ρR ππ vertices occurring in figures 1(a) and 1(b). As we did in [1] we shall
then use the gauge-invariance constraints to calculate the expressions for the diagram of
figure 1(c).
       Our paper is organized as follows.
    In section 2, we derive the new amplitudes for the DS term. In section 3, we present
a comparison of our tensor-pomeron model with data for the γp → π + π − p reaction
measured by the ZEUS [7] and H1 [8] Collaborations. In section 4 we summarize our
findings. In appendix A we compare our results to those presented by J. Pumplin in [9].

4   Conclusions

We summarize our findings.

    • We have in [1] given a calculation of the Drell-Söding (DS) term which fulfils all re-
      quirements of QFT. In this Addendum, we have updated the DS amplitudes taking
      into account pion off-shell effects in the Pππ, f 2R ππ, and ρR ππ vertices. We have
                                                                                ( a,b,c)
      considered the gauge invariance constraint for these new amplitudes Nµ             corre-
      sponding to the diagrams of figure 1. Using these constraints we could obtain an
                          (c)                            ( a)     (b)
      exact solution for Nµ , given the amplitudes Nµ and Nµ in the tensor-pomeron
      model. The complete result for our DS amplitude is given in (2.18). For the vector-
      meson production (ρ, ω) we used the formulas from section 2.2.1 of [1].

    • We have presented results of our model for real photoproduction γp → π + π − p and
      compared them with data from the ZEUS [7] and H1 [8] experiments. As can be
      seen from figures 2–6 our model gives a very satisfactory description of the data for
      invariant π + π − masses Mππ ≲ 1.2 GeV. For a description of the data for Mππ > 1.2
      GeV we would certainly have to include contributions of the higher mass mesons,
      e.g., ρ(1450), ρ3 (1690), and ρ(1700). This can be done with extensions of the meth-
      ods explained in section 2.1 of [2]. From our detailed study of distributions for
      Mππ ≲ 1.2 GeV we find from figure 3 that our tensor-pomeron model, incorporat-
      ing both resonant (ρ0 and ω) and non-resonant (DS) contributions, is in good agree-
      ment with the H1 experimental data. From figure 4 we see that the π + π − invariant
      mass distribution is skewed and the amount of skewing decreases with increasing
      |t|. Furthermore, our model with default parameters is also in good agreement with
      the ZEUS data; see figures 5 and 6.

    • In appendix A we compare our methods and results to those presented by J. Pumplin
      in [9].

    • We emphasize that in [1] and the present paper we are presenting a model for the
      complete amplitudes of the γ(∗) p → π + π − p reaction for real and virtual photons with
      0 ⩽ Q2 ≲ 0.5 GeV2 . Therefore, our model makes predictions for all measurable
      quantities in π + π − photoproduction and low Q2 electroproduction and, thus, al-
      lows for detailed comparison of theory and experiment.

    • We invite the experimentalists to make a complete and detailed comparison of our
      results for the γp → π + π − p reaction to the corresponding data from HERA. In this
      way we could get experimental numbers with errors for the (few) parameters of our
      model. These parameters refer to the couplings, intercepts, and slopes of the Regge
      trajectories P, f 2R , ρR , to be compared with other determinations, and to the shapes
      of form factors. Such an analysis would be very useful for the experimental study
      of central-exclusive production (CEP) of π + π − pairs in ultra-peripheral collisions
      of proton-proton scattering at the LHC, and in the future at electron-proton/ion
      collisions at EIC ([63–66] of [1]) and LHeC ([67, 68] of [1]).

    • We think that with the present paper we have made a large step in giving a QFT
      solution to a very old problem/puzzle. Given the shape of the Mππ distribution
      from the reaction e+ e− → ρ0 , ω → π + π − what is the shape of the corresponding
      distribution in π + π − photoproduction?

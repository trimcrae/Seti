                                                                             Abstract
                                                   We describe the image of a quantized BFN Coulomb branch 𝒜ℏ=1   𝐺,𝑁
                                               under localization (abelianization) map for any 𝐺, 𝑁 . In most cases,
                                               such as quiver gauge theories without loops, this description works for
                                               any flavors, sometimes we need to take generic or formal flavor param-
                                               eters. The answer is given by a roots and residue condition similar
                                               to Ginzburg—Kapranov—Vasserot construction of DAHA [GKV]. As a
                                               corollary, for any quantized conical Coulomb branch (and zero or small
                                               flavors) we provide a mathematical construction of the sphere trace in-
                                               troduced by Gaiotto and Okazaki [GO].

                          1 ℱ ⊗ 𝜋 𝒢 = 𝜋 (𝑝1 ℱ ⊗ 𝒢).

It remains to use the equation 𝑝2* 𝜋 * = 𝑞 * 𝑝2* . This equation means that
leaving only 𝑇 (𝑂) part of 𝐺(𝑂)-equivariant structure and then doing push-
forward is the same as doing pushforward and then leaving only 𝑇 (𝑂) part
of 𝐺(𝑂)-equivariant structure.
    The lemma follows.

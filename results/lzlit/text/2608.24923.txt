                                                                                                     Abstract
                                                         We identify a few small errors in the proof of Theorem 1 in M. Schuld, ‘Supervised quantum machine
                                                     learning models are kernel methods’ [1], Appendix A. These do not affect the validity of the theorem, but
                                                     do matter if one wants to use the appendix to explicitly compute the Fourier coefficients of a quantum
                                                     kernel. We give the corrected derivation, and additionally show that the paper’s worked cosine-kernel
                                                     example contains a second, unrelated error that happens to cancel the first, thereby coincidentally yielding
                                                     the correct result.

3   Comments on the cosine kernel example
We now look at the example presented in [1] using equation (A5) from the paper to derive the cosine kernel.

and conclude that in fact another error was made in the the computation that happened to cancel out the
error in equation (A5). We also show how using Equation 20 gives the correct answer. We start with using
Equation 20 with N = 1 and d = 2 and compare each step with the steps from Example A.1. in [1].
